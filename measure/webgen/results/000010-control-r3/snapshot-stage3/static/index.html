Spin the Wheel

Enter your name, spin the wheel, and see what you win. Every spin is saved to your records.

The Wheel

Enter your name, then press SPIN.

How to play

  • Type your name in the Your name box next to SPIN. A name is required.
  • Press SPIN and wait for the wheel to stop.
  • Your reward is saved automatically with your name and the date.
  • Check the Reward List to see every reward and its chance.
  • See all your wins on My Winning Records.

Your name is remembered on this browser.
